Changes and corrections

This project is corrected and extended over time. Every change that touches the physics is recorded here with its date.

How corrections work

When an error is found, the page is fixed and an entry below says what was wrong and what changed, crediting whoever spotted it if they would like that. Equation numbers are never reused: a corrected equation keeps its number, and its entry says so.

To report an error, email fbarinitsgr@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.

Versions

  • v0.3 · 9 November 2026

    Step 5, Open the box: the eternal black hole in equilibrium with a bath, Hawking’s growing entropy, the island formula, the island edge just outside the horizon, and the Page curve, following Almheiri, Mahajan and Maldacena.     Step 3, Throw something in: an exact shock-wave solution, the horizon moving before the pulse arrives, and negative energy making the wormhole traversable. Step 4, Two sides, one wormhole: the renormalised length and its Liouville dynamics. Conventions (C.11)–(C.16), and new rows in the Harlow–Jafferis table. No corrections to v0.1.

  • v0.1 · 9 November 2026

    First release. Contents page; step 1, AdS₂ is a box; step 2, Where is the black hole?; conventions, with a conversion table to Harlow and Jafferis.
