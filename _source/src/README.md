# A Black Hole in a Box

An interactive introduction to JT (Jackiw–Teitelboim) gravity, by [Faizan Bari](https://faizan-bari.github.io/).

**Live site:** https://faizan-bari.github.io/jt-gravity/

Zoom into the throat of a nearly extremal charged black hole and you find AdS₂ with one extra field, the dilaton. JT gravity is what is left of general relativity there: simple enough to solve exactly, rich enough to have horizons, wormholes and Hawking radiation. Every diagram on the site is drawn from an exact solution.

## Steps

1. **AdS₂ is a box**: free particles oscillate with the same period whatever their speed; light reaches infinity and comes back. *(live)*
2. **Where is the black hole?**: the geometry never changes; the black hole lives in the dilaton and the boundary. *(live)*
3. **Throw something in**: matter makes the horizon jump; negative energy makes the wormhole traversable. *(live)*
4. **Two sides, one wormhole**: the length between the two boundaries, and why it behaves like a particle bouncing off a wall. *(live)*
5. **Open the box**: a bath, Hawking’s growing entropy, the island and the Page curve. *(live)*

## How it is built

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step needed to view it. Maths is pre-rendered with [KaTeX](https://katex.org) (MIT licence), whose stylesheet and fonts are in `assets/katex/`.
