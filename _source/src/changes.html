Changes and corrections

This project is corrected and extended over time. Every change that touches the physics is recorded here with its date.

How corrections work

When an error is found, the page is fixed and an entry below says what was wrong and what changed, crediting whoever spotted it if they would like that. Equation numbers are never reused: a corrected equation keeps its number, and its entry says so.

To report an error, email fbarinitsgr@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
