Step 1 of 5

AdS₂ is a box

Anti-de Sitter space pulls everything back toward the middle. Throw a particle outward and it comes back, like a mass on a spring. Send light outward and it reaches infinity in a finite time, then returns.

The spacetime

AdS₂ is the two-dimensional spacetime with constant negative curvature, R=−2/ℓ2R=-2/\ell^2R=−2/ℓ2, where ℓ\ellℓ is the AdS radius. In global coordinates

ds2=ℓ2cos⁡2σ(−dτ2+dσ2),(1.1)ds^2=\frac{\ell^2}{\cos^2\sigma}\left(-d\tau^2+d\sigma^2\right), \tag{1.1}ds2=cos2σℓ2​(−dτ2+dσ2),(1.1)

where −π/2<σ<π/2-\pi/2<\sigma<\pi/2−π/2<σ<π/2 and τ\tauτ takes all real values. Apart from the overall factor this is flat space, so light still travels at 45° in the (σ,τ)(\sigma,\tau)(σ,τ) plane and the diagram below shows the causal structure faithfully. All of space fits in a strip of width π\piπ. Its edges are infinitely far away: the proper distance from the centre to σ\sigmaσ is ℓ artanh(sin⁡σ)\ell\,\mathrm{artanh}(\sin\sigma)ℓartanh(sinσ), which diverges as σ→±π/2\sigma\to\pm\pi/2σ→±π/2.

A harmonic trap

Near the centre, write x≈ℓσx\approx\ell\sigmax≈ℓσ for proper distance and t=ℓτ/ct=\ell\tau/ct=ℓτ/c for the time on a clock at rest there. To second order in xxx,

Compare with the weak-field metric, gtt≈−(1+2Φ/c2)c2g_{tt}\approx-(1+2\Phi/c^2)c^2gtt​≈−(1+2Φ/c2)c2: this is a Newtonian potential Φ=c2x2/2ℓ2\Phi=c^2x^2/2\ell^2Φ=c2x2/2ℓ2, a harmonic trap with angular frequency ω=c/ℓ\omega=c/\ellω=c/ℓ. So a slow particle oscillates with period 2πℓ/c2\pi\ell/c2πℓ/c.

The surprise is what happens far from the centre. Every freely falling massive particle follows

Everything refocuses

Take any event (τ1,σ1)(\tau_1,\sigma_1)(τ1​,σ1​). By (1.3), every free particle passing through it also passes through the mirror event (τ1+π, −σ1)(\tau_1+\pi,\,-\sigma_1)(τ1​+π,−σ1​), whatever its speed. So does light, if the edges reflect it. Two free-falling twins who part at one event meet again at the mirror event having aged by exactly the same amount, πℓ/c\pi\ell/cπℓ/c. Turn on the fan of launch speeds to see it.

The centre σ=0\sigma=0σ=0 is not a special place. AdS₂ is homogeneous: a symmetry can carry any free faller to the centre. Every path in the figure swings about σ=0\sigma=0σ=0 only because these coordinates were built around one particular free faller, the one at rest at σ=0\sigma=0σ=0.

Light reaches infinity

A light ray leaving the centre reaches the edge at Δτ=π/2\Delta\tau=\pi/2Δτ=π/2, a finite time, even though the edge is infinitely far away. What happens next is not decided by the equations inside. AdS needs a boundary condition at its edge, and with the standard reflecting one the light comes straight back. That is what makes AdS a box: nothing leaks out. In step 5 we will change this boundary condition and let radiation escape into a bath.

What this does not show

Everything here is a fixed AdS₂ with no dilaton yet, so there is no black hole and no boundary curve. Those come in step 2, on exactly this strip.

The facts on this page are standard. For AdS₂ in these coordinates see Harlow and Jafferis, arXiv:1804.01081, §2.1, where x=tan⁡σx=\tan\sigmax=tanσ.
